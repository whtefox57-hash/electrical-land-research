## 4. County-specific investigation for Priority candidates

For each Priority candidate, resolve the actual governing jurisdiction. Locate the county's official website and follow its GIS/assessor/planning links; a municipality or township may control land use. Verify publisher provenance for externally hosted services.

Inspect the actual map and available layer list, not merely the GIS landing page. Use its interface, legend, popups and downloadable records, or supported ArcGIS REST, WMS/WFS or other published data. Record what was actually viewed or queried. Cache reusable county adapters and metadata, but do not treat a cached county-level source as a completed parcel-level screen.

Inspect, where published and relevant: parcel/offered-part boundaries and acreage; jurisdiction; base zoning; future land use; zoning and special overlays; existing/pending cases; adopted/proposed restrictions and moratoria; public/private roads; maintenance responsibility; road rights-of-way; access routes; mapped utility/drainage/access easements; recorded plats/deeds and road instruments; floodplain/floodway and drainage; wetlands; slopes/soils; utility/water/service districts; airport/military/historic/conservation/hazard overlays; nearby residences and other incompatible uses.

Separate road visibility, public dedication, ownership of the underlying land, maintenance, frontage, driveway permission, and legally enforceable access. Never conclude that all easements were found because no easement layer exists. A GIS depiction is a screening signal; the recorded instrument, title review and survey answer different questions.

Use full-polygon intersections where valid geometry exists. A centroid/point query cannot clear the parcel. Capture layer/source URL, publisher, layer ID/name, effective date, retrieval time, CRS, query scope, coverage, complete result count, result status, relevant map capture/export, and evidence limitation. Use statuses VERIFIED_FINDING, NO_FEATURE_IN_COVERED_QUERY, NOT_PUBLISHED, NOT_REVIEWED, BLOCKED, CONFLICT, and NEEDS_INTERPRETATION. An authoritative source is not automatically conclusive for every claim.

A successful empty query is meaningful only when geometry, layer coverage, query filters, pagination and source completeness are known. Respect rate limits, licenses and access restrictions. Do not bypass logins or CAPTCHA. On failure, try lawful official alternatives, record the failure and specific next step, and continue other work.

Keep deed/assessor acreage, GIS-calculated area, scenario-based net usable area, and largest contiguous usable area separate. Validate geometry and units; subtract the union of overlapping exclusions rather than double-counting. Treat assumed road widths, setbacks and easement buffers as scenarios, not recorded rights. Do not claim a complete buildable envelope where constraints are missing.

For Watch/Rejected records, collect basic identity, location, source/date, acreage and price as reported, power signal, basic known restrictions, buyer/use hypothesis, alternate-use hypothesis, category rationale, unresolved issues and catalyst/reopening condition. No exhaustive overlay sweep unless later promoted or a narrow new fact is needed to assess reopening.
