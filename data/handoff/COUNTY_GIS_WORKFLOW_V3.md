# County GIS workflow v3 — initial screening before deep GIS

Prepared September 5, 2026, from the user's latest instruction. This supersedes the September 4 workflow's requirement to complete every county check before entry into the top research tier. This is a written specification, not evidence of a live task modification.

## Separation of decisions

A top research category is not an investment approval. Track independently: research tier (Priority / Watch / Rejected), investigation stage, source/evidence confidence, and capital authorization (normally HOLD during early screening).

## Routing

Discovery and basic file -> initial screening -> Priority with GIS_QUEUED -> official county/actual-jurisdiction deep review -> retain, downgrade, propose evidence-backed rejection, or record a blocked next step.

Watch/Rejected receive basic identity, source/date, geography, advertised acreage/price, infrastructure signal, preliminary fit/alternate use, known risks, rationale and next catalyst. Do not run full county-GIS audits on these categories unless re-promoted. A narrow recheck of a material new catalyst does not imply a full audit.

## Initial screen

Traceable asset/offering and resolved county; plausible use/scale/control economics; attributable public infrastructure or utility-process signal; buyer/use hypothesis; no supported fatal defect; low-cost decisive next test. Unknown formal utility capacity or title remains unknown without automatically preventing research prioritization. A node or ambiguous offered portion is not silently converted into a purchasable parcel.

## Deep review packet for promoted candidates

Official county homepage and relevant assessor/planning/GIS links; actual municipality/township authority; publisher-verified service metadata; full available layer inventory; exact parcel/offered-part geometry and CRS; zoning and future plan; mapped overlay restrictions and underlying code; public/private roads, ROW, dedication and maintenance; recorded plats, deeds and easement leads; flood/floodway, drainage, wetlands, terrain/soils, water/service districts, aviation/military/historic/conservation/hazards and neighboring incompatibilities where relevant.

Inspect map UI/legend/popups or lawfully query the underlying published services. Retain dates, source links, layer IDs, geometry/scope, coverage and pagination evidence, result status and count, captures/exports where permitted, uncertainty and contradictions. A retrieved landing page is not a completed parcel audit. A point query is not a full-polygon clearance.

Use distinct results: VERIFIED_FINDING; NO_FEATURE_IN_COVERED_QUERY; NOT_PUBLISHED; NOT_REVIEWED; BLOCKED; CONFLICT; NEEDS_INTERPRETATION. Zero results without confirmed coverage/completeness is not clearance. A road layer is not title or legal-access proof; missing easement geometry is not absence of an easement.

Where geometry permits, separate gross deed/assessor acreage, calculated GIS acreage, scenario-based net use and largest contiguous use area. Validate dimensions/CRS, subtract overlapping constraint unions once, and label assumed buffers. Do not calculate false precise buildable area from incomplete constraints.

## Handoff and learning

After review, save source-linked findings, changed gates, research score/version, evidence coverage and remaining risks. Generate calls only for specific gaps the available public record did not answer. Retain rejected claims separately from fatal physical defects. Archive versions and require new evidence to reopen. Source failures, missing geometry and tentative buyer fit must remain visible rather than being filled with plausible values.

No automated external messages, payments or contracts. No available-MW inference. $5,000 learning and $10,000 cumulative soft-control limits remain planning gates, not permission to spend.
