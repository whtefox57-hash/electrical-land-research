# County-first parcel research workflow — v2, September 4, 2026

## Mandatory ordering
Identify the exact legal offering → verify governing county/city → inspect official county GIS and layers → inspect governing code/plan and recorded maps → record conflicting/blocked/absent data → rank the next decisive test → make only a focused unresolved-question call → obtain written response → reassess before money.

## Required county source packet, for every parcel
Save the county homepage, assessor property record, parcel viewer, downloadable or REST data endpoints, zoning and future land-use maps, every available local overlay/special-district map, floodplain and floodway, drainage, road centerlines and road ROW/maintenance records, recorded easement/plats/deeds, pending planning cases, adopted and proposed rules, utility/water districts, airports/military/historic/conservation and other locally published constraints. Use the actual jurisdiction: a township or municipality may govern instead of the county.

Record publisher, source URL, effective date (or unknown), retrieval date, layer name/ID, coordinate reference, query geometry, geographic coverage, query/result count, result status, and limitations. Preserve source failures and conflicting versions. Do not mark a layer absent unless a valid, complete query returned no feature and coverage is known. A point screen does not clear a parcel polygon.

## Buildable-area discipline
Use sourced parcel and offered-portion polygons. Confirm polygon validity and coordinate reference before area work. Keep gross deed/assessor area, GIS area, net developable area and the largest contiguous construction envelope separate. Overlay constraints before subtracting their union to avoid double-counting overlaps. Treat suspected widths and buffers as explicit scenarios, not recorded rights. No current net-developable acreage has been calculated in this packet.

## Scoring and decision rules
Research priority is an uncalibrated decision aid, not probability or valuation. Unknown earns zero; critical missing evidence keeps spending on HOLD. A higher score means the next research test appears more worthwhile, not that a deal is likely to close. The live Priority lane additionally requires sourced parcel geometry, all nine core county topics at full-polygon scope, and a sufficient score. Do not manufacture green entries to fill a category.

Available load MW must stay UNKNOWN without exact-parcel/exact-load utility evidence. Generation MW, transformer size, line voltage, regional transmission upgrades and ordinary electric service are not substitutes. Title, access, seller authority, water rights, engineering and environmental conclusions require their own evidence.

## Permanent parcel file
Retain executive decision; legal asset; acreage conflicts; current/historical facts; county GIS audit; power/water/civil/customer/control questions; source ledger; scored assumptions; budgeted next actions; contacts; founder notes; complete decision history. Each APN gets its own file but shared assemblages are counted once as opportunities. Duplicate import must not duplicate IDs; updated records retain earlier full versions.

## Failure library
Record the failed strategy/claim separately from physical infeasibility. Require reason, supporting evidence, lesson, and a specific reopening condition. Retain unknowns as unknowns. No delete operation. Do not train the next screen on unverified negatives; compare dated predictions with later actual outcomes. The current six agricultural archive files retain the September 2 strategy decision; they are not fresh legal determinations or claims that the land has no other use.

## Calls and capital
Call only for a decision-sized gap after desktop work. Name the exact APN, source, discrepancy and document requested. A response must change a gate, supply a controlling document or identify the appropriate authority. No outbound calls/emails, study deposits, option fees or contracts were initiated by this build. Existing total founder expenditure was not reconstructed. Keep the existing $5,000 learning / $10,000 cumulative one-live-position limits; advanced diligence requires separate authorization and ideally sponsor funding.

## Implemented automation boundary
The hosted app has a bounded Cochise cadastral adapter, a Yuma cadastral-republication adapter and a Cochise service-catalog reader. They do not automate all overlays or all counties. Exact one-feature APN matching is required before copying a returned polygon. A successful cadastral fetch never clears title, easements, use, available MW or outstanding legal-offering conflicts. The existing hourly worker covers its public-source snapshot, not automatic county due diligence.
