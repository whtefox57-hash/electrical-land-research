# Buyer-first research inputs — October 29, 2026

Public-source developer profiles, offered-property research and independent matching review. Source URLs and retrieval dates are retained in each record. These are research snapshots, not live mandates or verified capacity.

Regenerate the application dataset and downloadable CSV/JSON/Markdown report with `python scripts/assemble-buyer-research.py`, then validate with `node scripts/validate-buyer-research.mjs`.

The assembly script records conservative exclusions, duplicate-offering merges, source conflicts, buyer-specific assumptions and forecast holds. Historical offering evidence is retained so a stale listing cannot silently re-enter the active pipeline.
