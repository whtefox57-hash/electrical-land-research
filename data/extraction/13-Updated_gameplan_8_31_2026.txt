VERDICT FIRST
Is the first step to identify a parcel?
No - not as an isolated act, and definitely not as the first place you spend money.
THE CORRECT STARTING SEQUENCE
In the first two weeks, run three workstreams in parallel: (1) a fixed-scope Arizona legal-structure review, (2) 8-12 buyer-discovery conversations that
produce a versioned requirement card, and (3) an automated node refresh. Parcel discovery begins immediately inside workstream 3, but a parcel is not
promoted to an option candidate until the first two workstreams have shaped the search.
Why parcel-first fails
A parcel can look attractive because it is near a substation, has industrial zoning, or is cheap. None of those facts proves the parcel fits a
buyer's live load, can be served on the buyer's schedule, or can be marketed lawfully through your contemplated option structure. The
prior Arizona screen proved the danger: 255 infrastructure candidates produced 166 private-land signals, but 248 had unknown capacity,
181 had overlapping utility territories, and the records were points rather than exact purchasable APNs. [P1-P8]
Start with a hypothesis, not certainty: a 10-25 MW first phase, a possible 50 MW expansion path, low-water cooling, 40-120 usable
acres, and an Arizona location are the initial search box until buyers correct it.
Start the parcel pipeline now, but label every result NODE LEAD ONLY until exact APN, owner, boundary, jurisdiction and access are
supported by official records.
Do not pay an option fee until counsel approves the intended assignment/marketing structure, the seller can authorize diligence, the
exact serving-utility intake path is identified, and at least one buyer class plausibly fits.
The initial business objective is not to prove that a site is powered. It is to create a cheap, exclusive and transferable right to investigate
and present one unusually relevant site to funded counterparties.
CURRENT VERDICT
CALL SPRINT / HOLD MONEY. Up to $2,500 may be used for a fixed-scope legal memo, essential parcel/title data and limited demand discovery.
Recommended current spend is $0 for an option fee, generic electrical report or formal utility study.

WHAT THE COMPANY ACTUALLY DOES
The option-led assignment model in one sentence
THE MODEL
Find a buyer-shaped parcel, secure cheap and assignable conditional control, assemble only the evidence that changes the next decision, and transfer
the contractual position to a funded developer before Synchronicity must close on the land or finance major utility and field studies.
What you own
A time-limited contractual right against a specific seller, with access, inquiry and cooperation rights.
A permissioned, dated evidence room tied to exact APNs and a defined load hypothesis.
A record of which buyer objections, utility processes and local constraints were verified or remain unknown.
What you do not own
Utility capacity, a queue position, an energization promise, an entitlement, a water supply or buyer interest.
The right to market another person's land as an agent, unless the structure and conduct comply with applicable licensing law.
Professional engineering, title, environmental, water or legal conclusions merely because you summarized them.

LEVERAGE TEST
If a buyer can replace your parcel cheaply, the option is weak. Leverage comes from a scarce combination of control, timing, exact buyer fit, credible
process evidence and a seller price that leaves room for the buyer - not from the word 'option.'
