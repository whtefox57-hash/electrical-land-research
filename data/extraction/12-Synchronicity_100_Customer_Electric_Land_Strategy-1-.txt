Executive decision
What the research says before you spend meaningful capital
The service company is viable enough to test with $5,000 or less. The land-option/flip thesis is not presently financeable or solo-founder
compatible. Your product is reduced uncertainty, not acreage near a line.
1
The best wedge is narrow and early.
Sell Gate 2 parcel kill screens, Gate 3 human validation, Gate 7 political monitoring and Gate 9 evidence passports. Do not sell grid-capacity conclusions, engineering,
brokerage or a vague 'site selection' bundle.
2
One hundred simultaneous customers is impossible.
Even two hours per account per month would consume 2,400 hours per year. A disciplined solo cap is 2 new one-offs per month or 8 recurring accounts, with no more than
3 live projects.
3
Cold enterprise conversion is the weak link.
The row-level priors sum to about 1.8 paid pilots across this 100-name list and roughly $19k of first-pilot billings. That is an expectation, not a floor; zero wins is plausible.
4
The economics work only with upfront cash and pass-through experts.
Require 60% at signature, 40% on delivery, and 100% prepayment of title, counsel, travel, engineering and environmental work. Never fund a customer's diligence from
your balance sheet.

A sales process one founder can execute
The sequence is evidence-first and stops before free consulting
Touch
Timing
Action
What not to do
1

2

Send one public-record fact they may have missed and explain the decision it could change.

Day 60-90

Recommended first offer language
We find the reason not to spend the next $100,000. In five business days, Synchronicity delivers an exact-parcel decision record covering
APN/ownership, zoning, flood, access, utility territory, water and fiber proxies, public approvals and unresolved questions. The output is
stop, proceed or escalate - with every statement linked to a source. Fixed fee: $9,500; 60% at signature; expert work and travel prepaid
separately.
Contract terms that preserve the model
- Fixed deliverable, named parcel/market, five-day clock after complete inputs, and one 30-minute readout.
- 60% non-refundable kickoff payment; 40% due on delivery; late fees and pause rights.
- Client owns decisions; report is not engineering, utility commitment, legal, environmental, title or investment advice.
- External experts contract directly with client where possible; otherwise client prepays 100% and accepts their terms.
- No success fee, land spread, brokerage representation, option marketing or percentage of financing without local counsel and any required license.
- Mutual confidentiality; no hyperscaler or tenant name in marketing without written permission; secure evidence room and deletion schedule.

Honest viability evaluation
What could work, what probably will not, and the thresholds that decide
Dimension
Assessment
Evidence and implication
Customer pain
REAL
Power, entitlement and community constraints make bad-site diligence expensive. The provided Arizona work found capacity unknown for 248 of 255 infrastructure
candidates and utility assignment ambiguous for 181 of 255 - exactly the uncertainty a human-verified evidence product addresses.
Willingness to pay
PLAUSIBLE, NOT PROVEN
Avoided diligence is worth far more than a $7.5k-$20k fee, but sophisticated firms already employ internal teams and trusted consultants. You need paid pilots, not
compliments, to prove the wedge.
Solo delivery
VIABLE WITH LIMITS
Gates 0-3, 7-9 can be productized. Gates 5-6 require experts. Gate 10 and full development are incompatible with one founder.
Competitive undercut
VIABLE ON ENTRY SCOPE
You can offer a lower initial ticket and faster decision than a broad consulting engagement. You cannot sustainably underprice licensed engineering, title, legal or
environmental work.
Sales efficiency
WEAK
The modeled cold list produces about 1.8 expected pilots and $19k of first-pilot billings. A single warm channel can be more valuable than 50 additional cold names.
Capital risk
LOW IF DISCIPLINED
The service model can be tested under $5k and made cash-positive with upfront payment. Land options, speculative studies and self-funded experts would reverse that
advantage.
Defensibility
LOW AT FIRST; CAN IMPROVE
A generic memo is commoditized. Defensibility comes from a consistent evidence schema, local call history, source/expiry tracking, expert relationships and customer trust.
Founder credibility
MATERIAL RISK
A 22-year-old law student will not win by presenting as a power or engineering expert. The credible position is disciplined researcher/orchestrator with transparent limits and
named senior reviewers.
Overall
CONDITIONAL GO
Run a 90-day paid-pilot test. Continue only if you close at least one $7.5k-plus project, receive 60% upfront, deliver within 30 founder hours and obtain a reference or
referral.
